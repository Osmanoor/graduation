### KEY POINTS

*   **Mark Allocation:** The examiners allocate 10 out of 60 marks specifically for the formatting and presentation of the thesis document.
*   **General Body Formatting:**
    *   Font size: 12 pt.
    *   Font type: Times New Roman.
    *   Style: Unbold.
    *   Line spacing: 1.5.
    *   Alignment: Justified ("نعمل ضبط جوانب يكون justified").
*   **Headings Formatting:**
    *   Main Chapter Titles (e.g., "Chapter 1 introduction"): Font size 18, bold, and centered.
    *   Subtitles (e.g., "1.1 preface"): Font size 16, Times New Roman, bold. (She notes a general range of 14, 16, up to 18 for titles).
*   **Cover Page Requirements:**
    *   **Mandatory:** You must strictly follow the department's standard template ("ما بتعمل صفحة غلاف من راسك"). If the coordinator ("المنسقة") sends a specific template, it must be used.
    *   Project Title: Font size 20, Times New Roman, bold.
    *   The word "thesis" (or similar phrase): Must be italicized in a specific font.
    *   Required Information: Students' names, index numbers, supervisor's name, and the date.
    *   Required Text: Must exactly include "submitted to Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, University of Khartoum".
    *   Logo: It is a recent trend to include the university logo on the cover page.
    *   Page Numbering: The cover page counts as page 'i' (Roman), but the number must **not** be visible on the page itself.
*   **Preliminary Pages (Front Matter):**
    *   Must be placed in a separate section with Roman numeral numbering (i, ii, iii...).
    *   Includes: dedication, acknowledgment, declaration, abstract (in English), مستخلص (abstract in Arabic), table of contents, list of figures, list of tables, and list of abbreviations.
*   **Chapter 1 (Introduction) Content & Rules:**
    *   Content is similar to the proposal: includes a general introduction, problem definition, and objectives.
    *   **Mandatory:** Do *not* include the full methodology and results in Chapter 1; these belong in later chapters.
    *   **Objectives:** You are allowed to modify the objectives from the proposal stage. It is **mandatory** ("لازم" / "يجب") that the objectives are "very specific", point directly to the methodology used, and prove that the problem defined was solved.
    *   **Literature Review / Related Work:** While it is technically allowed to include "state of art" or "related work" in Chapter 1, it is highly **preferred** ("يستحسن") to place this in a dedicated Chapter 2 ("literature review") for better organization.
    *   **Thesis Outline / Layout:** It is **mandatory** ("لازم نختمه") to end Chapter 1 with an outline of the thesis structure.
        *   Must explain the organization to the reader (e.g., "this thesis is organized in five chapters...").
        *   Can be written as one long paragraph or multiple paragraphs, but it is **preferred** ("يستحسن") to be written as continuous text.
