### KEY POINTS

*   **Panel Composition:** The defense panel consists of the project supervisor and two examiners from the department's professors.
*   **Defense Format:** Both students in a project group will enter the same panel and defend together (a change from older rules where students wrote separate theses and defended individually).
*   **Presentation Division:** Students must divide the presentation roles. For example, one student presents the introduction and problem definition, the other explains the methodology, and the first returns to present the results and discussion.
*   **Presentation Flow:** It is highly important that the transition between the two students is very smooth to demonstrate harmony within the team.
*   **Mandatory Comprehensive Knowledge:** It is mandatory ("لازم") that each student fully understands the entire project, including the parts their partner worked on or is presenting. They must not focus only on their own sections.
*   **Targeted Questioning:** Examiners may direct questions specifically to one student. If the student cannot answer, it will result in a negative mark in their evaluation ("نقطة سالبة في تقييمه").
*   **Document Navigation:** Examiners can ask students to open a specific page in the report to explain a specific figure, table, or the analysis of a result.
*   **Mandatory Material:** It is mandatory ("لازم") for students to have a copy of the thesis with them during the defense.
*   **Submission Guidelines:** 
    *   Historically, to ease the burden on students, they were allowed to submit soft copies and print only one shared hard copy (even if it had "two different covers").
    *   Currently, students must strictly follow the instructions of the coordinator ("المنسق") regarding submission formats.
    *   If the coordinator requires one soft copy and one hard copy, the students must bring the hard copy to the defense. If the coordinator says only soft copies are required, students must follow that directive.
