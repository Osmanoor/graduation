### KEY POINTS

**Defense Format & Logistics:**
*   The thesis writing phase is complete; the focus is now on the presentation.
*   She is currently unaware of the official format for the final defense (whether it will be a pre-recorded submission like the past two years, an in-person defense "حضورية", or an online defense).

**Software & Tools:**
*   There is no mandatory software requirement ("مافي اشتراط محدد").
*   PowerPoint is the most common and expected tool, though other tools like Prezi are acceptable.

**Presentation Structure & Content Requirements:**
*   **Slide 1 (Title Slide):** Must include the project name in a large font ("اسم المشروع كبير"), the student's name, and the supervisor's name.
*   **Slide 2 (Agenda):** This is mandatory ("لازم يكون فيها الـ agenda"). It must outline the sequence of topics to be discussed. She reminds students to refer to a previously sent recorded lecture titled "how to write your presentation".
*   **Core Sections:** The presentation must briefly cover the following in order:
    1.  Introduction / Problem Definition (She notes this is the most important: "اهم حاجة").
    2.  Objectives.
    3.  Methodology (Focus strictly on what was actually done in the research; be "to the point").
    4.  Results and Discussion.
    5.  Conclusion.

**Formatting & Presentation Style:**
*   The presentation is not a report; text must be kept to an absolute minimum ("ما قل ودل").
*   Students must rely heavily on graphics/drawings ("الرسومات") and bullet points ("bullets").
*   The actual explanation of the slides must be delivered verbally ("verbal").

**Strict Rules on Theory (Warning):**
*   Do not elaborate on theories ("ما نسهب في الـ theories").
*   Theoretical background must be strictly limited to 1 or 2 slides maximum ("slide او two slides فقط").
*   *Reasoning:* The examiners already know the theories. Spending too much time on theoretical parts wastes time ("بتضيع الزمن"). If time runs out, examiners will interrupt and force the student to skip straight to the results ("يقولوا ليك لا خلاص كفاية امشي خلاص للنتائج"), meaning the student will lose the chance to properly explain their own methodology and hard work.
*   The vast majority of the presentation time must be focused on the student's actual work: the methodology, the results, the analysis of those results, and the conclusion.
