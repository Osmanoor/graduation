### KEY POINTS

*   **Chapter 5 Naming:** The chapter is typically titled "conclusion, comments and recommendations" or "recommendations for future work", though the exact title is not strictly mandatory ("الاسم ما شرط").
*   **Chapter 5 Content:** It must generally contain general comments on the project, the results, the conclusion, and the recommendations.
*   **Importance of Recommendations:** Writing recommendations is highly emphasized ("مهمة جداً") as it demonstrates the student's comprehensive understanding of the project and its limitations.
*   **Formulating Recommendations:** Students should include new visions for solving the problem or new methods discovered while working on the project.
*   **Defense Preparation (Justification):** Students must be prepared to explain exactly *why* they did not implement the features or solutions they are suggesting in their recommendations ("وتكون عارف إنك إنت ليه ما عملت الحاجة اللي إنت أصلاً وضعتها في توصية").
*   **Purpose of Recommendations:** They must be written to serve as a guide ("هادية") for future students who might replicate the project differently or continue the current work.
*   **Evaluation/Grading Indicator:** The clarity and strength of the recommendations directly prove to the evaluators that the student understands what they did, the results achieved, and how the project can be improved.
