### KEY POINTS

*   **Presentation Preparation:** Students must review a previously sent lecture regarding how to deliver a presentation.
*   **Non-verbal Communication Rules:**
    *   Maintain **eye contact** with everyone present in the room.
    *   Pay strict attention to **body language** ("لغة الجسد"), as it is mandatory to be aware that every movement has a specific interpretation ("لازم يعني عندها تفسير معين").
    *   Stop moving too fast/pacing ("بطل الحركة السريعة").
    *   Do not put your hands in your pockets ("ما تدخل يديك في جيوبك").
    *   Avoid awkward or inappropriate standing postures ("ما تقيف وقفة كده").
*   **Handling the Q&A (Content):**
    *   The content ("المحتوى") of the answers is explicitly stated as more important than general presentation skills.
    *   If a student does not understand a question, it is completely acceptable to ask the professor to repeat it ("ممكن تطلب من الأستاذ يعيد السؤال مرة تانية عادي مافي مشكلة").
    *   Students must fully understand the question to provide a logical, scientific answer ("الإجابة العلمية المنطقية").
*   **Evaluation/Marking Warnings:**
    *   Giving a careless/nonsense answer ("إجابة أي كلام") or an answer that is **off point** will result in a negative mark/deduction ("تؤخذ نقطة سالبة").
    *   Such answers will reflect poorly on the student's final evaluation ("في التقييم بتكون يعني نقطة سيئة بالنسبة ليك").
