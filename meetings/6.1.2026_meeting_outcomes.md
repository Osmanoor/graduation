## Category 1: Architecture Decisions

### 1.1 Baseline Retrieval Approach
**AI Assumed:** Hybrid (Dense + BM25) as single baseline  
**Actual Decision:** ✅ **Test All Three Separately**

- **Dense Retriever** - Test independently to see its contribution
- **BM25 (Sparse)** - Test independently to see its contribution  
- **Hybrid** - Optional third comparison if time permits

**Rationale:** Testing separately gives more insight into where improvements come from. Papers often test both Dense and BM25 effects independently. Hybrid is industry practice but doesn't give us unique insights.

**Quote from meeting:** "نحن نشوف تأثيرهم في الـ Dense كيف، وتأثيرهم في الـ BM25 كيف" (We see their effect on Dense and on BM25 separately)
