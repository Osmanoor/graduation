## Detailed Findings

### 1. GPT-OSS Training Data (Task 4.1)
OpenAI's official model card and the technical report for GPT-OSS (arXiv:2508.10925) state that the models were trained on a "text-only dataset with trillions of tokens, with a focus on STEM, coding, and general knowledge." The report explicitly acknowledges that "general-purpose pretraining alone is insufficient for robust multilingual capability," noting accuracy drops below 45% on Chinese tasks and weak performance on the ILMAAM Arabic benchmark (~58%).

### 2. mDPR Query Length Distribution (Task 4.10)
Citable statistics for MS MARCO, the dataset used to train mDPR, show an average query length of 6 words (Guangyuan Ma et al., 2023; Nguyen et al., 2016). This supports the thesis argument that the dense encoder's performance degrades when processing the much longer (~1,500 character) expansions generated by CSQE.

### 3. CSQE Improvement Verification (Task 4.5)
In the CSQE paper (Lei et al., 2024), Table 7 shows that for the TREC DL19 benchmark, BM25+CSQE using Llama2-Chat-7B achieved an mAP of 39.1. Compared to the BM25 baseline of 30.1 (Table 3), this represents an improvement of:
$$\frac{39.1 - 30.1}{30.1} \approx 29.9\%$$
This matches the "30% improvement" cited in the thesis.
