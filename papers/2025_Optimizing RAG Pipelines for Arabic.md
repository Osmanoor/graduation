- **Short Description:** This study presents a comprehensive empirical evaluation of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) components tailored specifically for the Arabic language. By benchmarking various chunking strategies, embedding models, rerankers, and language models, the authors provide optimized configurations for building high-quality Arabic RAG pipelines.

- **Research Question:** The authors aim to determine the optimal combination of pipeline components—specifically chunking strategies, embedding models, reranking mechanisms, and large language models—required to maximize retrieval precision and generation faithfulness in Arabic RAG systems, addressing the linguistic complexities of the language.

- **Main Methodology:** The study employs a modular experimental pipeline implemented via LangChain to systematically isolate and evaluate four core components: chunking (fixed-size, recursive, sentence-aware, and semantic), embedding (comparing six multilingual and Arabic-specific models), reranking (using `bge-reranker-v2-m3`), and generation (comparing Aya-8B against StableLM-1.6B). Performance is assessed using the RAGAS framework, which automates the evaluation of retrieval and generation quality.

- **Dataset & Benchmark:** The evaluation utilizes six diverse Arabic datasets: **ARCD** and **ArSQUAD** (QA/Reading Comprehension), **SaudiWiki** (Structured Encyclopedic), **QA4MRE** (Inference/Reasoning), **Quran Tafseer** (Religious/Semantically Dense), and **Hindawi Books** (Literary/Unstructured). The system is benchmarked using four core RAGAS metrics: Context Precision, Context Recall, Answer Faithfulness, and Answer Relevancy.

- **Research Contributions:**
    This work fills a significant gap in Natural Language Processing by providing the first systematic analysis of RAG pipeline components specifically for Arabic, a morphologically complex language often underrepresented in RAG research. Unlike studies focused on high-resource languages, this research empirically validates that **sentence-aware chunking** is the superior segmentation strategy for Arabic, consistently outperforming semantic and recursive methods by effectively balancing context integrity with retrieval precision across diverse text types.

    Furthermore, the study offers critical technical insights for implementation, demonstrating that high-capacity multilingual embedding models (specifically **bge-m3** and **multilingual-e5-large**) outperform specialized Arabic-only models in retrieval tasks. The authors also establish that integrating a reranker significantly boosts answer faithfulness in complex reasoning scenarios (such as the QA4MRE dataset) and identify **Aya-8B** as the superior generator for semantically dense Arabic content compared to StableLM.
