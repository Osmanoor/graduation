## 7. Claims that could not be verified from repository sources

1. **Row 1, broadened scope.** The A5 edit widened the Arabic gap claim from "no prior study has evaluated **sub-7B models for zero-shot** QE in monolingual Arabic retrieval" to "**LLM-based QE** has not been evaluated for monolingual Arabic retrieval" — i.e. it now covers every model scale and every prompting regime. The repo's targeted literature search was scoped to sub-7B models (`research_decisions/WS6_RESEARCH_REPORT.md:16`, Task 6.1 heading "Post-2024 small-LLM (<7B) Arabic QE papers"); the only general-scope evidence is `family1_corpus_aware_query_expansion_analysis.md:543-549`, which cites Arabic QE work from 2013 and 2017. The claim is probably still true, but its *widened* form has not been searched for and should be re-verified before submission.
2. **Macmillan-Scott et al. (2025).** No PDF or summary of arXiv 2511.19325 is present in `papers/`. Its characterisation as cross-lingual-only, and the corroboration claims attached to it at `chapter2.tex:446`, rest entirely on the second-hand notes in `research_decisions/WS6_RESEARCH_REPORT.md:35-47`. That note also records that the paper evaluates **Exp4Fuse and MuGI across eight languages including Arabic**, which is further reason to soften row 2's "unexamined in any language".
3. **MuGI (`zhang_2024_mugi`).** No paper summary in `papers/`. The description at `chapter2.tex:438` (adaptive repetition scaled to generated-text length) was checked only against the thesis's own methodology section and project notes, not against the paper.
4. **Exp4Fuse (`liu_2025_exp4fuse`).** No PDF or summary in `papers/`; the "single sparse retriever" characterisation underpinning gap 4 comes from `research_decisions/hybrid_retrieval_qe_literature_review.md:44-51` only.
5. **ThinkQE, KAR, AQE, `zhang_2025_pbr`, `yoon_2025_llm_retrieval`.** No summaries in `papers/`. The gap-1 claim (row 4) was verified against the CSQE paper and the GaQR/RQ-RAG descriptions; the remaining citations at `chapter2.tex:440` were not independently checked.
6. **"Temperature 0.1 … +2.5\% over temperature 0.7"** (`chapter4.tex:433`) sits inside the retitled section but was not part of the A5 diff and was not verified against experiment logs.
