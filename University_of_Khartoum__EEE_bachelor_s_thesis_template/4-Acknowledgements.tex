\chapter*{Acknowledgements}

We wish to express our sincere gratitude to our supervisor, Dr.~Tahani Abdalla Attia,
for her guidance, her patience, and her willingness to read this work as carefully as
she asked us to write it. Her insistence on clarity, structure, and evidence shaped
this thesis more than any single technical decision within it.

We are grateful to the academic and technical staff of the Department of Electrical
and Electronic Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, University of Khartoum, for their
teaching and for keeping the department running under conditions that made doing so
genuinely difficult.

We also thank our fellow students for their friendship and for the discussions,
technical and otherwise, that made a solitary piece of research feel considerably less
solitary.

This work would not have been possible without the open research community. Every
language model evaluated in this thesis was released publicly by its developers, the
MIRACL benchmark was made openly available for research use, and every experiment
reported here was run on freely provided cloud computing resources. A study of this
scope came within reach of two undergraduate students only because of that openness.

Finally, we thank our families, whose support was constant and unconditional
throughout. Whatever this thesis is worth, a large share of it is theirs.
