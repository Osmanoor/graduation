\chapter*{Dedication}

To Sudan.

To a country that has taught its children to keep building while the ground shakes
beneath them. To those who have lost their homes, their universities, and the people
they love, and who carry on regardless; to the doctors, the teachers, and the
volunteers who stayed; and to every student who kept a notebook open through a war
that offered every reason to close it. This work was written in the conviction that
Sudan will need its engineers.

To our families, who carried more of this degree than we did---for the years of
patience, for the sacrifices that were never mentioned, and for a confidence in us
that never wavered, including through the long stretches when we could not summon it
ourselves.

To our friends, and to the batch we started with---for the late nights, the shared
panic before every deadline, and the humour that made all of it survivable.

To the University of Khartoum, and to the teachers who kept its lecture halls alive
through displacement and disruption rather than let a generation go untaught.

And to everyone who asked how the project was going and stayed to hear the
answer---this belongs to you as well.
